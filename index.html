Little questions. Clear answers.

How does prescription-to-cart work?

Upload a clear JPG, PNG or PDF and choose human or pet care. We read the written medicine names and match exact catalogue items. Review the matches and quantities, then add them to your cart. Unclear items stay unmatched.

Can I get medicines and a doctor in 10 minutes?

That’s the Healio goal: quicker everyday medicine delivery and doctor access for humans and pets. Availability depends on your location and the care needed. This preview prepares requests; it does not confirm delivery or a medical appointment.

How do the tree rewards work?

Every 10 completed orders earns one tree planted for you. You also earn 5 points for every ₹100 spent. Redeem 50 points to plant an additional tree. Points are credited after a completed order, and planting is confirmed separately.

Can I compare prices before adding to my cart?

Yes. Open Compare on any product to see its price alongside other retailers. The current catalogue uses clearly labelled sample prices; check the retailer for its current offer, stock, delivery and pack size.

Is pet care separate from human care?

Yes. The care shelf and prescription uploader separate human and pet products. Veterinary prescription products require review. Never swap human and pet medicines or substitute one pet species’ medicine for another’s.
